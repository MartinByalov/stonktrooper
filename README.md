## Welcome to the market

**Stonktrooper** is a financial arcade runner where the stock chart is your
obstacle course. Jump across candlesticks, throw briefcases at enemies, collect
pips, and try not to get liquidated.

Bulls, bears, whales, and black swans stand between you and a new best score.
Expect market chaos, internet memes, and questionable decisions.

- **Survive the chart.** Navigate market gaps, double-jump zones, and special encounters.
- **Make an impact.** Your briefcase means business. So does your fedora.
- **Chase your best score.** Collect coins and diamonds, then share your result with friends.
- **Day or Night.** Pick your theme and enjoy a satirical Live Feed along the way.
- **Play anywhere.** Desktop or mobile browser, with touch controls and landscape gameplay on mobile.

About Stonktrooper

Stonktrooper is a game inspired by fictional financial markets, internet memes and experimental game design. It is an amalgamation of the author's experience with the real markets.

The game is free to play. There are no purchases, subscriptions, paid upgrades or real-money gameplay. Signing in with Google is optional, and your progress is then kept in your own Google Drive.

Play Stonktrooper in your browser on a computer or a phone.

How to play

You run along a chart of candlesticks that never stops. The chart has gaps, enemies and things to pick up, and your score is counted in pips.

  • Jump to avoid the gaps and to collect pickups.
  • Use the highlighted zones on the chart to double jump.
  • Aim and shoot ahead with your suitcase.
  • When you find a hat, throw it. It makes a fabulous weapon, and your enemies may like your style.

On a computer you play with the keyboard. On a phone you hold it sideways in fullscreen and use the touch zones on the screen.

What you will meet

  • Coins and diamonds to collect. Hold on to the diamonds.
  • Bulls and bears, and riders on their mounts. Shoot them or stomp them. The first hit drops the rider and the next hit takes the mount.
  • Whales, which you cannot jump on. Shoot one for a sure reward or a small chance of a ride to the moon.
  • Pigeons that bring the money printer, hawks that offer tendies, and apes with bananas.
  • Swans, the bag holders. Hit one and you are left holding the bag, so you cannot shoot for a while.
  • Bosses that join the ride when the bell rings.

Style and progress

What you collect becomes currencies that you spend in the Exchange on candles, hats, suitcases, suits, shoes, pets and mounts. There are achievements, weekly quests and a leaderboard. Once a month a shared event called Monthly Squeeze asks all players to reach a common goal for a reward everyone receives.

Guest or Google

You can play as a Guest without an account, and your game stays in your browser. If you sign in with Google, the game saves your progress to a hidden app data folder in your own Google Drive, so you can continue on another device. The game does not ask for your name, your email address or your profile picture. In Preferences you can disconnect your Google account at any time. Read more in the Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions.

Not financial advice

Prices, charts, headlines, characters and events in the game are fictional. Nothing in Stonktrooper is investment, financial or trading advice.
